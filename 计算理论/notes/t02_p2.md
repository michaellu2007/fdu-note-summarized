### 第 25 页

**I. Formal Definition of DFA**

NFA $M = (K, \Sigma, \Delta, s, F)$.

To construct an equivalent DFA $M' = (K', \Sigma, \delta, s', F')$.

- Let $K' = 2^K$
- Let $s' = E(s)$
- Let $F' = \{Q \mid Q \subseteq K, Q \cap F \neq \emptyset\}$
- For each $Q \subseteq K$ and $\forall a \in \Sigma$,
  Let $\delta(Q, a) = \cup\{E(p) \mid p \in K \text{ and } (q, a, p) \in \Delta \text{ for some } q \in Q\}$

### 第 27 页

**II. NFA $\Rightarrow$ DFA construction: verification**

（方框内）We next show:

- $\underline{M'}$ is deterministic
- $\underline{M'}$ is equivalent to $\underline{M}$.

---

### 第 28 页

- **M' is deterministic:**

  - $\delta$ is a single-valued and well defined on all $Q \in K'$ and $a \in \Sigma$ by the way it is constructed.

  - For some $Q \in K'$ and $a \in \Sigma$, $\delta(Q, a) = \emptyset$ does not mean $\delta$ is not well defined; $\emptyset$ is a member of $K'$.

---

### 第 29 页

- $M'$ **is equivalent to** $M$.

**Claim:** For any string $w \in \Sigma^*$ and any states $p, q \in K$,

$$(q, w) \vdash_M^* (p, e) \iff (E(q), w) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e)$$

for some set $P$ containing $p$.

Consider any string $w \in \Sigma^*$.

$$w \in L(M) \iff (s, w) \vdash_M^* (f, e) \text{ for some } f \in F \quad \text{[definition]}$$
$$\iff (E(s), w) \vdash_{M'}^* (Q, e) \text{ for some } f \in Q \quad \text{[claim]}$$
$$\iff (s', w) \vdash_{M'}^* (Q, e) \text{ with } Q \in F'$$
$$\iff w \in L(M')$$

From the claim, $M'$ is equivalent to $M$.

---

### 第 30 页

- **Proof of Claim:**

**Basis step:**

For $|w| = 0$, i.e. $w = e$, we must show that

$$(q, e) \vdash_M^* (p, e) \iff (E(q), e) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e)$$

for some set $P$ containing $p$.

（红色方框）

$$(q, e) \vdash_M^* (p, e) \iff p \in E(q)$$

（红色方框）

$$\text{$M'$ is deterministic}$$
$$(E(q), e) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e) \iff P = E(q)$$

$$p \in P \Rightarrow p \in E(q)$$

---

### 第 31 页

**Induction hypothesis:**

Suppose the claim is true for all string $w$ of length $k$ or less for some $k \geq 0$.

**Induction step:**

Let $|w| = k + 1$. $w = va$, where $a \in \Sigma, v \in \Sigma^*$.

$\Rightarrow$（红色方框）

$$(q, w) \vdash_M^* (p, e) \iff (E(q), w) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e)$$

$$(q, w) \vdash_M^* (p, e) \iff \exists \text{ states } r_1 \text{ and } r_2 \text{ such that}$$
$$(q, va) \vdash_M^* (r_1, a) \vdash_M (r_2, e) \vdash_M^* (p, e)$$

---

### 第 32 页

**I**

$$(q, va) \vdash_M^* (r_1, a) \iff (q, v) \vdash_M^* (r_1, e)$$
$$\iff (E(q), v) \vdash_{M'}^* (R_1, e) \text{ for some } R_1, r_1 \in R_1$$

（红色方框）**Induction hypothesis**

**II**

$$(r_1, a) \vdash_M (r_2, e) \iff (r_1, a, r_2) \in \Delta$$
$$\Rightarrow E(r_2) \subseteq \delta(R_1, a)$$

（红色方框）**Definition of $\delta(R_1, a)$**　　（云朵气泡）$p \in \delta(R_1, a)$

**III**

$$(r_2, e) \vdash_M^* (p, e) \Rightarrow p \in E(r_2)$$

$$\Rightarrow (E(q), va) \vdash_{M'}^* (R_1, a) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e).$$

（方框）$P = \delta(R_1, a)$

---

### 第 33 页

（$\Leftarrow$ 方向）

- $(E(q), va) \vdash_{M'}^* (R_1, a) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e)$ for some $p \in P$
- $(R_1, a) \vdash_{M'}^* (P, e) \Rightarrow P = \delta(R_1, a)$
- $\delta(R_1, a) = \cup\{E(r_2) : \exists r_2 \in K \text{ and } (r_1, a, r_2) \in \Delta \text{ for some } r_1 \in R_1\}$

$$p \in P \Rightarrow \exists r_2 \text{ such that } p \in E(r_2) \text{ and for some } r_1 \in R_1, (r_1, a, r_2) \in \Delta$$

- By IH,

$$(q, va) \vdash_M^* (r_1, a)$$

Therefore,

$$(q, va) \vdash_M^* (r_1, a) \vdash_M^* (r_2, e) \vdash_M^* (p, e).$$

### 第 37 页

（关系图）

### 第 38 页

$\square$ **Closure Properties of Regular Languages**

**Theorem:** The class of languages accepted by FA is **closed** under

(a) Union
(b) Concatenation
(c) Kleene star
(d) Complementation
(e) Intersection

**Proof:**

（红框）**Main idea** To construct an FA that accept the appropriate language in each case.

---

### 第 39 页

**(a) Union:**

Let $M_i = (K_i, \Sigma, \Delta_i, s_i, F_i)$ $(i = 1, 2)$ be two NFA.

$\Rightarrow$ construct a NFA $M = (K, \Sigma, \Delta, s, F)$, such that

$$L(M) = L(M_1) \cup L(M_2).$$

Where

- $K = K_1 \cup K_2 \cup \{s\}$
- $F = F_1 \cup F_2$
- $\Delta = \Delta_1 \cup \Delta_2 \cup \{(s, e, s_1), (s, e, s_2)\}$

（右侧示意图：新增初态 $s$，两条 $e$ 边分别指向子自动机 $M_1$ 与 $M_2$。）

**Note:** Any finite union of regular sets is regular. Inifinite unions may not be regular.

---

### 第 40 页

**(b) Concatenation:**

Let $M_1 = (K_1, \Sigma, \Delta_1, s_1, F_1)$ and $M_2 = (K_2, \Sigma, \Delta_2, s_2, F_2)$ be two NFA.

$\Rightarrow$ construct a NFA $M = (K, \Sigma, \Delta, s, F)$, such that

$$L(M) = L(M_1) \circ L(M_2).$$

（示意图：$M_1$ 的终态经一条 $e$ 边连到 $M_2$ 的初态。）

Where

- $K = K_1 \cup K_2$
- $F = F_2$
- $\Delta = \Delta_1 \cup \Delta_2 \cup \{(q_i, e, s_2) : q_i \in F_1\}$

---

### 第 41 页

**(c) Kleene star:**

Let $M_1 = (K_1, \Sigma, \Delta_1, s_1, F_1)$ be a NFA.

$\Rightarrow$ construct a NFA $M = (K, \Sigma, \Delta, s, F)$, such that

$$L(M) = L(M_1)^*.$$

（示意图：新增初态 $s_1'$（同时为终态），$s_1' \xrightarrow{e} s_1$；$M_1$ 的各终态经 $e$ 边回到 $s_1$。）

Where

- $K = K_1 \cup \{s_1'\}$
- $F = F_1 \cup \{s_1'\}$
- $\Delta = \Delta_1 \cup \{(q_i, e, s_1) : q_i \in F_1\} \cup \{(s_1', e, s_1)\}$

---

### 第 42 页

**(d) Complementation:**

Let $M_1 = (K_1, \Sigma, \delta_1, s_1, F_1)$ be a **DFA**.

$\Rightarrow$ construct a DFA $M = (K, \Sigma, \delta, s, F)$, such that

$$L(M) = \overline{L(M_1)} = \Sigma^* - L(M_1).$$

Where

- $K = K_1$
- $s = s_1$
- $F = K - F_1$
- $\delta = \delta_1$

---

### 第 43 页

**(e) Intersection:**

（红色方框）

$$L(M_1) \cap L(M_2) = \Sigma^* - ((\Sigma^* - L(M_1)) \cup (\Sigma^* - L(M_2)))$$

Let $M_i = (K_i, \Sigma, \delta_i, s_i, F_i)$ $(i = 1, 2)$ be two DFA.

$\Rightarrow$ construct a DFA $M = (K, \Sigma, \delta, s, F)$, such that

$$L(M) = L(M_1) \cap L(M_2).$$

Where

- $K = K_1 \times K_2$
- $s = (s_1, s_2)$
- $F = F_1 \times F_2$
- $\delta : K \times \Sigma \rightarrow K$
  $$\delta((q_1, q_2), a) = (\delta_1(q_1, a), \delta_2(q_2, a))$$

（云朵气泡）"parallel" simulation!

（红框）How about $L(M_1) \cup L(M_2)$?

### 第 45 页

$\square$ **FA and Regular Express are equivalent**

**Theorem:** A language is regular iff it is accepted by a FA.

**Proof:** $\Rightarrow$

（红色方框）The class of regular languages is the smallest class of languages containing $\emptyset$ and the singletons $\{a|a \in \Sigma\}$, and closed under **union**, **concatenation**, and **Kleene star**.

（红色方框）$\emptyset$ and the singletons $\{a|a \in \Sigma\}$ are accepted by FA.

$\Rightarrow$ every regular language is accepted by some FA.

---

### 第 46 页

（$\Leftarrow$ 方向）

（方框）Let $M = (K, \Sigma, \Delta, s, F)$ be a FA.
To construct a regular expression $R$, s. t. $L(M) = L(R)$.

Let $K = \{q_1, \cdots, q_n\}$ and $s = q_1$.

We'll do induction over $k$ for executions that use only states in $\{q_1, \cdots, q_k\}$

（右侧示意图：一个椭圆区域标注 "States numbered $\le k$"，其中包含 $q_1$ 与 $q_i$，并有一条虚线路径连到区域外的 $q_j$。）

---

### 第 47 页

**Definition:** For $i, j = 1, \cdots, n$ and $k = 0, \cdots, n$, define $R(i, j, k)$ as the set of all strings in $\Sigma^*$ that may drive $M$ from state $q_i$ to state $q_j$ without passing through any intermediate state numbered $k + 1$ or greater.

（红色方框）

$$R(i, j, 0) = \begin{cases} \{a \in \Sigma \cup \{e\} \mid (q_i, a, q_j) \in \Delta\} & \text{if } i \neq j \\ \{a \in \Sigma \cup \{e\} \mid (q_i, a, q_j) \in \Delta\} \cup \{e\} & \text{if } i = j \end{cases}$$

**Note:**

$$R(i, j, n) = \{\, w \mid w \in \Sigma^*, (q_i, w) \vdash_M^* (q_j, e) \,\}$$
$$L(M) = \cup\{\, R(1, j, n) \mid q_j \in F \,\}$$

---

### 第 48 页

**Theorem:** $R(i, j, k)$ are regular languages.

**Proof:** By induction on $k$.
**Basis step:** $k = 0$

$$R(i, j, 0) = \begin{cases} \{a \in \Sigma \cup \{e\} \mid (q_i, a, q_j) \in \Delta\} & \text{if } i \neq j \\ \{a \in \Sigma \cup \{e\} \mid (q_i, a, q_j) \in \Delta\} \cup \{e\} & \text{if } i = j \end{cases}$$

**Induction step:**

$$R(i, j, k) = R(i, j, k - 1) \cup R(i, k, k - 1) R(k, k, k - 1)^* R(k, j, k - 1)$$

$\Rightarrow R(i, j, k)$ is regular for all $i, j, k$.
