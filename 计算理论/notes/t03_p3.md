### 第 47 页

**II. Building a CFG from a PDA**

**Lemma:** If a language is accepted by a PDA, it is CFL.

**Outline of the Proof**

- Define the simple PDA
- Convert a PDA to an equivalent simple PDA
- Building a CFG from the simple PDA

---

### 第 48 页

**Definition of Simple PDA**

A PDA is **simple** if the following is true:
Whenever $((q,a,\beta),(p,\gamma))$ is a transition of the PDA and $q$ is not the start state, then $\beta\in\Gamma$ and $|\gamma|\le 2$.

In other words, A simple PDA always

- consults its topmost stack symbol, and
- replace it either $e$, or with single stack symbol, or with two stack symbols.

---

### 第 49 页

**Convert a PDA to an equivalent simple PDA**

Let $M=(K,\Sigma,\Gamma,\Delta,s,F)$ be any PDA,
$\Rightarrow$ construct a simple PDA $M'=(K',\Sigma,\Gamma\cup\{Z\},\Delta',s',\{f'\})$ such that $L(M)=L(M')$.

- $s',f'\notin K$ be two new states, $Z\notin\Gamma$ be the stack bottom symbol.
- $K'=K\cup\cdots$
- $\Delta'$ contains:
  - the transition $((s',e,e),(s,Z))$ (**start transition**)
  - for each $f\in F$, $((f,e,Z),(f',e))$ (**final transition**)
  - all transition of $\Delta$
    — replace with equivalent transitions that satisfy the simplicity condition.

---

### 第 50 页

**Replace transitions with equivalent transitions that satisfy the simplicity condition:**

- **Get rid of transitions with $\beta\ge 2$:**

Consider any transition $((q,a,\beta),(p,\gamma))\in\Delta'$, where $\beta=B_1\cdots B_n$, with $n>1$.

Replace with the following transitions:

$$((q,e,B_1),(q_{B_1},e))$$
$$((q_{B_1},e,B_2),(q_{B_1B_2},e))$$
$$\vdots$$
$$((q_{B_1\cdots B_{n-2}},e,B_{n-1}),(q_{B_1\cdots B_{n-1}},e))$$
$$((q_{B_1\cdots B_{n-1}},a,B_n),(p,\gamma))$$

---

### 第 51 页

- **Get rid of transitions with $\gamma>2$, without introducing any transitions with $\beta\ge 2$.**

Consider any transition $((q,u,\beta),(p,\gamma))\in\Delta'$, where $\gamma=C_1\cdots C_m$, with $m\ge 2$.

Replace with the following transitions:

$$((q,u,\beta),(r_1,C_m))$$
$$((r_1,e,e),(r_2,C_{m-1}))$$
$$\vdots$$
$$((r_{m-2},e,e),(r_{m-1},C_2))$$
$$((r_{m-1},e,e),(p,C_1))$$

$r_1,\dots,r_{m-1}$ are new states.

---

### 第 52 页

- **Get rid of transitions with $\beta=e$, without introducing any transitions with $\beta\ge 2$ or $\gamma>2$.**

Consider any transition $((q,a,e),(p,\gamma))$ with $q\ne s'$.

Replace any such transitions by all transitions of the form

$$((q,a,A),(p,\gamma A)) \quad \text{for all } A\in\Gamma\cup\{Z\}$$

It is easy to see that $L(M)=L(M')$.

### 第 53 页

**Construct A CFG from a simple PDA**

$\Rightarrow$ Construct a CFG $G=(V,\Sigma,R,S)$ such that $L(G)=L(M')$.

- Let's look at how a PDA can consume $x=x_1x_2\cdots x_k$ and empty the stack.

**图示（曲线图）说明：**
纵轴自下而上标出 $y_1,y_2,\dots,y_k$，横轴被分成 $k$ 段，依次为 $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_k$。
曲线从左端 $p_0$ 出发，在每一段 $x_i$ 上方起伏，中间经过 $p_1,\dots$（用省略号连接），右端到 $p_{k-1}$，最终到达 $p_k$。
即 PDA 每读入一段 $x_i$，栈高度 $y_i$ 相应变化，读完全部 $x$ 后栈被清空。

| 记号 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| $p_0,p_1,\dots,p_k$ | 曲线（栈高轨迹）在各段的顶点/转折点 |
| $y_1,y_2,\dots,y_k$ | 对应的栈高度 |
| $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_k$ | 输入串 $x$ 的分段 |

### 第 54 页

**Definition:**

The **nonterminals** $\langle q,A,p\rangle$:
represents any portion of the input string that might be read between a point when $M$ is in state $q$ with $A$ on top of stack, and a point in time when $M$ removes the occurrence of $A$ from the stack and enters state $p$.

- $V=\{S\}\cup\Sigma\cup\{\langle q,A,p\rangle \mid \forall q,p\in K,\ A\in\Gamma\cup\{e,Z\}\}$

---

### 第 55 页

**图示说明：** 左侧为状态 $q$ 的方框，其栈顶符号为 $A$；右侧为状态 $p$ 的方框，其栈已弹出 $A$。两者之间以 $\vdash^*$ 相连。下方两条纸带均为 $a\,b\,b\,a\,b$，用括号标出从"处于状态 $q$、栈顶为 $A$"到"进入状态 $p$ 并弹出 $A$"之间所读入的那一段输入。

图注文字：

> *portion of input read from when M is in state q with A on stack to when M enters state p and pops A.*

| 元素 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| 左方框 $q$ + 栈顶 $A$ | 起始时刻：$M$ 处于状态 $q$，栈顶为 $A$ |
| 右方框 $p$ + 栈（$A$ 被弹出） | 终止时刻：$M$ 进入状态 $p$ 且弹出 $A$ |
| $\vdash^*$ | 中间的若干步计算 |
| 纸带 $a\,b\,b\,a\,b$ | 输入串；下划线标注区间为期间读入的部分 |

### 第 56 页

- The rules in $R$ are of four types.

(1) The rules $S\to\langle s,Z,f'\rangle$:
— $s$ the start state of original PDA and $f'$ the new final state.

(2) For each transition $((q,a,B),(r,C))$, where $q,r\in K$, $a\in\Sigma\cup\{e\}$, $B,C\in\Gamma\cup\{e\}$, and for each $p\in K$, we add the rule $\langle q,B,p\rangle\to a\langle r,C,p\rangle$.

(3) For each transition $((q,a,B),(r,C_1C_2))$, where $q,r\in K$, $a\in\Sigma\cup\{e\}$, $B\in\Gamma\cup\{e\}$, and $C_1,C_2\in\Gamma$ and for each $p,p'\in K$ we add the rule $\langle q,B,p\rangle\to a\langle r,C_1,p'\rangle\langle p',C_2,p\rangle$.

(4) For each $q\in K$, the rule $\langle q,e,q\rangle\to e$.

---

### 第 57 页

**Claim:** For any $q,p\in K$, $A\in\Gamma\cup\{e\}$, and $x\in\Sigma^*$,

$$\langle q,A,p\rangle\Rightarrow_G^* x \iff (q,x,A)\vdash_M^* (p,e,e)$$

The claim will suffice to Lemma.

$$\langle s,Z,f'\rangle\Rightarrow_G^* x \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad (s,x,Z)\vdash_M^* (f',e,e)$$
$$\Big\downarrow \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad \Big\downarrow$$
$$x\in L(G) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad x\in L(M)$$

### 第 58 页

**3.5 Languages that are and are not Context-Free**

**Closure Properties**

**Theorem:** The CFL are closed under union, concatenation, and Kleene star.

**Proof:**

Let $G_1=(V_1,\Sigma_1,R_1,S_1)$ and $G_2=(V_2,\Sigma_2,R_2,S_2)$ be two CFG.

Without loss generality, assume that $(V_1-\Sigma_1)$ and $(V_2-\Sigma_2)$ are disjoint.

---

### 第 59 页

**a) Union**

Let $G=(V_1\cup V_2\cup\{S\},\Sigma_1\cup\Sigma_2,R,S)$, where
$R=R_1\cup R_2\cup\{S\to S_1,\ S\to S_2\}$
Then $L(G)=L(G_1)\cup L(G_2)$.

**b) Concatenation**

Let $G=(V_1\cup V_2\cup\{S\},\Sigma_1\cup\Sigma_2,R,S)$, where
$R=R_1\cup R_2\cup\{S\to S_1S_2\}$
Then $L(G)=L(G_1)L(G_2)$.

---

### 第 60 页

**c) Kleene star**

Let $G=(V_1\cup\{S\},\Sigma_1,R,S)$, where
$R=R_1\cup\{S\to e,\ S\to SS_1\}$
Then $L(G)=L(G_1)^*$.

**Remark:**
CFLs are not closed under intersection and complement.

### 第 61 页

**Theorem:** The Intersection of a CFL with a regular language is a CFL.

**Proof:**

Build a new machine that simulates both automata.

**图示说明：**
- 上方为 **Read-only tape**（只读纸带），带内容为 $a\ a$，有一个 **Tape head**（带头）指向纸带。
- 左侧为 **Stack**（栈），栈底符号为 $Z$。
- 下方一个红框内同时放置 **PDA** 与 **DFA** 两个方框；带头读取的输入同时送入 PDA 与 DFA，PDA 另与 Stack 相连。

| 组件 | 说明 |
| --- | --- |
| Read-only tape | 输入纸带（如 $a\,a$） |
| Tape head | 读头，同时向 PDA 与 DFA 提供输入 |
| Stack（栈底 $Z$） | 仅由 PDA 使用 |
| PDA + DFA | 红框内两自动机并行运行、同时被模拟 |

Let $M_1=(K_1,\Sigma,\Gamma_1,\Delta_1,s_1,F_1)$ be a PDA and $M_2=(K_2,\Sigma,\delta,s_2,F_2)$ be a DFA.

Build PDA $M=(K,\Sigma,\Gamma,\Delta,s,F)$, where

  (1) For each $((q_1,a,\beta),(p_1,\gamma))\in\Delta_1$, and $q_2\in K_2$,
  $$(((q_1,q_2),a,\beta),((p_1,\delta(q_2,a)),\gamma))\in\Delta$$
  (2) For each $((q_1,e,\beta),(p_1,\gamma))\in\Delta_1$, and $q_2\in K_2$,
  $$(((q_1,q_2),e,\beta),((p_1,q_2),\gamma))\in\Delta$$

### 第 63 页

**Example:**

$$L=\{w:\ w\in\{a,b\}^*,\ w \text{ has equal numbers of } a\text{'s and } b\text{'s but containing no substring } abaa \text{ or } babb\}.$$

Then $L$ is context-free.

**Solution:**

$L_1=\{w:\ w\in\{a,b\}^*,\ w \text{ has equal numbers of } a\text{'s and } b\text{'s}\}$
　　[旁注：$L_1$ be a CFL accepted by a PDA]

$L_2=\{w\in\{a,b\}^*:\ w \text{ containing no substring } abaa \text{ or } babb\}.$
$\quad = \{a,b\}^* - \{a,b\}^*(abaa\cup babb)\{a,b\}^*$.
　　[旁注：regular]

$L=L_1\cap L_2$ be a context-free language.

### 第 64 页

**Pumping Theorem**

**Lemma** The yield of any parse tree of $G$ of height $h$ has length at most $\phi(G)^h$.

**图示说明：** 一个倒三角（三角形）表示以 $S$ 为根的解析树。树中有一条加粗的 **Path**（路径）从根 $S$ 蜿蜒向下延伸到底边。根处标注 **Fanout of $G=\phi(G)$**（每个结点的最大扇出为 $\phi(G)$）。右侧标注 **Height = h**（树高为 $h$）。底边下方标注 $\phi(G)^h$ **symbols**（叶子即产出串长度为 $\phi(G)^h$）。

| 标注 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| Fanout of $G=\phi(G)$ | 文法 $G$ 每个结点的最大分支数（扇出） |
| Path | 从根到叶的一条路径 |
| Height = h | 树高 $h$ |
| $\phi(G)^h$ symbols | 产出（yield）的最大长度 |

---

### 第 65 页

**Proof:** The proof is by induction on $h$.

**Basis step:** $h=1$
（图示：一个三角形，根为 $S$，下方标注 $\phi(G)$ symbols）

**Induction step:**
（图示：根为 $S$，向下分出若干子树，标注 **at most $\phi(G)$ subtrees**；每棵子树高 $h-1$，各自产出不超过 $\phi(G)^{h-1}$ 个符号，整体下方标注 $\phi(G)^{h-1}$ symbols）

| 步骤 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| Basis step | $h=1$：产出为 $\phi(G)$ 个符号 |
| Induction step | 根 $S$ 有 at most $\phi(G)$ 棵子树；每棵子树高 $h-1$，产出 $\phi(G)^{h-1}$ 个符号 |

---

### 第 66 页

**Theorem (Pumping Theorem)** Let $G=(V,\Sigma,R,S)$ be a CFG. Then any string $w\in L(G)$ of length greater than $\phi(G)^{|V-\Sigma|}$ can be rewritten as $w=uvxyz$ in such way that

- $|vy|\ge 1$
- $uv^nxy^nz\in L(G)$ for every $n\ge 0$.

**Proof**

- Let $w$ be such a string.
- Let $T$ be the parse tree with root labeled $S$ and with yield $w$ that has the smallest number of leaves.

---

### 第 67 页

$$|w|>\phi(G)^{|V-\Sigma|}$$

$\Rightarrow$ The height of $T>|V-\Sigma|$ (by Lemma)

$\Rightarrow$ $\exists$ a path of length at least $|V-\Sigma|+1$, with at least $|V-\Sigma|+2$ nodes.

$\quad$ (only one labeled by terminal, the remaining are labeled by nonterminal).

$\Rightarrow$ $\exists$ two nodes on the path labeled with the same symbol.

---

### 第 68 页

**图示说明：**
- 左图 $T$：以 $S$ 为根的三角形，内部有一个以非终结符 $A$ 为根的子树 $T'$，$T'$ 内又嵌套一个同样以 $A$ 为根的子树 $T''$。底边被划分为 $u\ v\ x\ y\ z$ 五段（$x$ 对应 $T''$ 的产出，$v,y$ 对应 $T'$ 相对 $T''$ 多出的部分）。
- 右图：把子树 $T'$ 复制多次后的树，底边为 $u\ v\ y\ z$（其中一段内部又嵌套 $v\ x\ y$），说明 $T'$ 可被重复替换。
- 箭头处文字：**The subtree $T'$ can be replicated multiple times.**

| 片段 | 对应子树 |
| --- | --- |
| $u$ | $S$ 到 $T'$ 左侧之前的产出 |
| $v$ | $T'$ 相对 $T''$ 左侧多出的产出 |
| $x$ | $T''$ 的产出 |
| $y$ | $T'$ 相对 $T''$ 右侧多出的产出 |
| $z$ | $T'$ 右侧之后的产出 |

正文：

If $vy=e$, then there is a parse tree with root $S$ and yield $w$ with fewer leaves.

But $T$ is the smallest tree of this kind.

—**contradiction!**
